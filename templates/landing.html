Estimates you can trust to be honest

A machine-learning model estimates your next period from your own history. It shows a likely window instead of a single promised date, tells you how confident it is, and says so when you have not logged enough cycles. It was trained on a public research dataset, and in testing it was only slightly better than a plain average of your recent cycles - we show you that too.
