How the model works

  • It looks at the gaps between your last cycles, their average and how much they vary.
  • With 3 or more usable cycles it uses a regression model (ridge regression) saved with Joblib.
  • With fewer cycles it uses the average of the cycles you have, and labels the estimate low confidence.
  • The range comes from the model's real errors on people it had never seen.

Limits

  • Trained on the Kaggle FedCycle dataset: adults charting for fertility awareness, median age about 31 - not Indian college students.
  • Cycles that vary a lot are harder to predict for any method.
  • Cycles longer than 60 days or shorter than 15 days are left out of the estimate (but never deleted).
  • Never use this for contraception or fertility planning.
